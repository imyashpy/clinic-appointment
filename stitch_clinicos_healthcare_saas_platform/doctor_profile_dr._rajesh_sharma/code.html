Clinical Focus & Ethos

Holistic Cardiovascular Care Powered by Diagnostic Clarity

Dr. Rajesh Sharma is a pioneer in evidence-based preventive cardiology and minimally invasive radial-approach coronary interventions. Having spent over 16 years treating complex acute coronary syndromes and hypertension at apex institutes like AIIMS New Delhi and Mount Sinai, Dr. Sharma combines cutting-edge catheter technology with actionable lifestyle medicine.

His consultative approach strictly avoids premature stenting, prioritizing guideline-directed lipid pharmacotherapy, autonomic conditioning, and personalized risk stratification using CT Calcium scoring and advanced lipoprotein profiling.
